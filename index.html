The boundary

Spreader Math keeps bag, refill and walking arithmetic only. It gives no verdicts about dial settings or what your spreader should be set to - that call belongs to the spreader's manual and the bag's label. Follow the product label exactly; the label is the law of the lawn.

The bag promises 2,500 square feet. Your lawn has other plans. Spreader Math does the honest arithmetic: how many bags the lawn really takes, how many times you walk back to the bag, and how long the laps actually run.

Three panels. The bag truth divides your lawn by the claimed coverage. The refill truth turns the label rate into pounds and pounds into hopper fills. The walk truth multiplies swath and pace into the honest length of the job.
